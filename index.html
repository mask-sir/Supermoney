IMPORTANT

Video KYC Instructions

After creating your FD, you may need to complete Video KYC to finish the verification process.

  1. Confirm your name when asked.
  2. Confirm your mother's name.
  3. Confirm your date of birth.
  4. Confirm your name again if requested.
  5. Look straight at the camera while they take your picture.
  6. Switch to the back camera when instructed and show your original PAN card. Capture a clear picture of it.
  7. Sign on a plain white sheet of paper.
  8. Show the signature and capture it when instructed.
  9. Wait for confirmation that your Video KYC is completed.

Don't panic about the language!

Video KYC may be conducted in English or Hindi. The representative will ask about details you have already provided. Stay calm, listen carefully, and answer the questions with the requested details. You can simply say "OK" when appropriate.

You're Almost Done!

After completing Video KYC, your card will be ready within 24 hours, subject to the bank's verification and approval process.
